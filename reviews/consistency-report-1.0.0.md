## 6. Narrative pages

### 6.1 Language and editorial conventions

**General conventions to apply across the pages:**

- **[6.1-1]** **Spelling.** Pages mix British and US English ("organisation" / "organization", "realise" / "organize"). HL7 Europe normally uses British English.
- **[6.1-2]** **Naming of the eHN guidelines.** Use the full name from `references.md` once, then "eHN HDR Guidelines".
- **[6.1-3]** **Naming of the Joint Action.** Always write "Xt-EHR Joint Action".
- **[6.1-4]** **Punctuation.** Don't mix hyphens and dashes (`challenges.md:4`), or straight and curly apostrophes (`authors.md:14`).
- **[6.1-5]** **Markup.** Remove the leftover `<head>/<title>` elements inside `<div>` fragments in the map pages and `modelmap.xml`. Also remove the mojibake comment (`âœ…`) in `modelmap.xml:7`, and the empty `<a> </a>` and `<p></p>` elements in the notes include.
